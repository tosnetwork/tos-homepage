Intent Commerce

From revocable Intent to accountable action.

Humans and Agents publish or exchange Intents, interpret them locally, negotiate exact terms, and form an Agreement before consequential work begins. The Agreement binds authority, resources, evidence, recourse, and the selected settlement domain without forcing private payloads or every interaction on-chain.

  1. 01 Intent

    Express a revocable outcome, opportunity, or need with provenance and revision.

  2. 02 Discover & negotiate

    Interpret locally, compare candidates, exchange offers, and clarify material terms.

  3. 03 Agree & authorize

    Accept an exact revision that binds parties, scope, price, authority, evidence, and recourse.

  4. 04 Execute & prove

    Perform bounded work once under stable action identity and return agreed evidence.

  5. 05 Settle & reconcile

    Complete direct, escrow, Gift, or external settlement—or dispute or refuse—under the Agreement.

TO

Intent is not authority

An Intent, ranking, conversation, model inference, or UI action cannot silently become an Agreement, payment permission, or execution mandate.

BA

Bounded authority

Capabilities, controller keys, spending limits, expiry, delegation depth, and revocation constrain autonomous software.

MR

Minimum sufficient institution

Use direct trust for low-risk reversible work, stronger authenticated evidence across organizations, and enforceable settlement only when the risk requires it.

RR

Agreement-bound evidence

Signed records connect stable actions to acceptance, accounting, refunds, disputes, and the selected settlement authority without publishing private payloads.

TB

Transport bindings

The same operation can bind to HTTP, MCP, A2A, OpenAPI, WebSocket, QUIC, or RLDP. Transport success is not authority or settlement success.

Web2 gave machines APIs. TOS gives agents economic opcodes.

The missing layer is shared meaning around consequential action.

Interfaces explain how to connect. Payment rails explain how value moves. TOS proposes portable Intent, Agreement, authority, action-identity, evidence, Receipt, dispute, and settlement semantics. An independent implementation can use those semantics without using OpenFox, a TOS SDK, or a TOS-operated marketplace.

Interface layer Connect

HTTP, MCP, A2A, and OpenAPI carry or describe requests and interactions.

Payment layer Transfer

Stablecoins and payment-bearing requests move value between a buyer and provider.

Agreement layer Commit

Exact parties, revisions, authority, deliverables, evidence, recourse, and payment terms travel together.

Provider boundary Execute

Private payloads, models, tools, data, and physical control stay under provider and owner policy.

Unit of value

An Agreement-bound outcome

Value attaches to an exact accepted commitment and its evidence, not an isolated API call, model story, Gateway row, or unauthenticated message.

Open by design

Complement, do not capture

Public semantics should travel across existing runtimes, interfaces, discovery systems, and payment methods without turning one implementation into mandatory infrastructure.

Defensibility

Integration and verified history

Provider conformance, portable receipts, reliability data, and third-party SDK adoption must compound without forcing agents into a closed marketplace.
The settlement thesis must be earned.

TOS Core is selected only when its common state is needed and its measured cost, finality, recovery, developer experience, and neutrality justify that choice. Direct trust or another settlement domain remains valid when the Agreement selects it. Architecture is a hypothesis; repeat external use is evidence.

Institutional choice

Use the lightest mechanism that safely fits the relationship.

Markets, organizations, direct trust, authenticated Agreements, arbitration, external payment, and blockchain escrow all impose different costs. TOS must not force an on-chain transaction merely to demonstrate decentralization.

DT

Direct trust

Trusted parties may perform and pay directly, including an explicitly voluntary Gift, while accepting the documented risk.

AE

Authenticated evidence

Signed Agreements and evidence carry exact commitments across organizations without placing every payload or interaction on-chain.

ES

Enforceable settlement

TOS escrow or another accepted mechanism is appropriate for higher-value, lower-trust, contested, or irreversible obligations after its prerequisites pass.

RF

Refuse safely

When authority, legality, custody, evidence, or recourse is insufficient, refusal or an inconclusive result is safer than invented certainty.

Discovery finds possibilities. Agreement creates obligations.

TOS is designed for compatibility with Agentic Resource Discovery and plural, independently deployable registries. Discovery output remains untrusted input: it can identify a candidate, but only exact acceptance and authorization can create an Agreement or consequential action.

The next market underneath the network

Agents can transact. Accountable owners retain authority.

An Agent can spend or earn only within authority delegated by an accountable owner or organization. A capable implementation can discover an offer, form an exact Agreement, and use its selected payment path without asking a human about every low-risk action. This is the target model; production autonomy and external demand still require acceptance.

Bounded agency

An Agent can act without becoming the owner.

Agent Accounts distinguish owner authority, delegated controller authority, balances, and spending policy. The Agent acts only within that bounded mandate; it does not become the owner or obtain unrestricted keys.

Plural commerce

The Agreement chooses how value moves.

Per-call, fixed-price, subscription, direct, Gift, escrow, or external payment can be selected by an exact Agreement. A payment event proves only what its declared authority establishes; it does not prove delivery, acceptance, profit, or legal ownership by itself.

Verifiable claims

Control and provenance are not automatic ownership.

Before a resource is Agent-reachable, an accountable party must describe it, identify the asserted controller or provider, define terms and permissions, and expose evidence appropriate to the claim. TOS may anchor selected commitments; it does not turn every description or rights claim into legal ownership.

Four steps make a capability eligible for bounded Agent use.

Turning a Skill, dataset, model, service, or device into something an Agent may use requires four accountable steps. Discovery and payment alone never establish trust, authority, quality, or ownership.

01

Publish a versioned claim

Describe the Skill, dataset, model, service, or device in a machine-readable revision with source, terms, permissions, and evidence expectations. A listing is a claim to verify, not authority to act.

02

Bind it to a verifiable identity

Bind the service claim to a verifiable identity, authority domain, provenance, and capability grant. A chain record proves the adopted claim or control state, not automatic legal ownership of physical, intellectual, or off-chain property.

03

Set policy and exact terms

Define allowed callers, budgets, approvals, limits, price, evidence, recourse, and settlement choices. The capability remains unavailable until the owner policy and a compatible Agreement admit it.

04

Record evidence without universalizing trust

A conforming Agreement can require signed evidence and a Receipt. Verified history may inform future local judgment, but it does not become universal reputation or guarantee future value.

Machine-readable access creates a candidate market offering—not automatic trust or ownership.

A useful offering combines an accountable controller, versioned description, bounded interface, owner policy, exact Agreement, appropriate evidence, and a selected settlement path. Legal rights, service quality, delivery, and profitability still require their own evidence.

Target operating model—not a claim of autonomous production commerce today.

Agent Account and the current tos_service_v1 registry and commerce engineering exist with local evidence. Fresh current-domain public-testnet authority, independent commercial acceptance, recurring external demand, and production custody remain pending. OpenFox has a bounded same-host eight-Agent campaign, not a deployed earnings service or proof of external profit.

Separate implemented engineering from public and production acceptance.
