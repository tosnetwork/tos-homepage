Simplex consensus.
QUIC transport.

Validators authenticate proposals and votes, form weighted quorum certificates, and advance notarization and finalization. The canonical profile selects Simplex v2 over QUIC.

400 ms is the configured target block interval, not a measured end-to-end finality or throughput guarantee. Actual behavior depends on the network and its active configuration.

Execution is message-driven.

TVM executes contract code over cells with deterministic gas accounting. A transaction has distinct compute, action, and bounce behavior. Cross-contract work spans messages and must handle replies and failures explicitly.
