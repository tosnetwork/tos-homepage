Intelligence is leaving the data center.

Open-weight models in the 8-14B range now run directly on a Mac mini, a gaming PC, a home NAS, or a Jetson-class box, capable enough for tool calling, coding, and retrieval, not just chat. That turns a growing population of ordinary owner-operated devices into potential agent nodes, not just endpoints.

Naming compatible hardware, not announcing a rewards program.

Home and site relays remain planned work, and a storage service profile is scoped for a later roadmap phase. Buying either device does not connect it to TOS today. It identifies the class of owner-operated hardware this architecture is designed to admit once that work ships.

Distributed model capacity without surrendering core data.

TOS coordinates independently operated model services while sensitive workloads can remain on customer-controlled local, private, or on-premise nodes. The network settles evidence and value; it does not require central custody of proprietary datasets.

Local-first execution

Sensitive tasks can be restricted to approved local, private, or edge nodes. Routing moves outward only when customer policy permits it.

Minimum necessary disclosure

Service descriptors expose capabilities and terms, not unrestricted access. Task policy limits payloads, tools, endpoints, budgets, and retention.

Proof without publishing secrets

Private prompts, datasets, and outputs stay off-chain. Signed receipts, hashes, and evidence references provide accountability without publishing the underlying content.

No single-company chokepoint

Plural providers, registries, and owner-operated nodes reduce dependence on one company, region, or platform policy. If one route fails or refuses service, agents can discover policy-compatible alternatives.

Designed to reduce data exposure, not to make an impossible absolute guarantee.

Security still depends on node hardening, encryption, access control, trusted runtimes, and operator practice. Highly sensitive workloads should remain on explicitly approved customer-controlled nodes.

Not a bet against any model provider.

TOS does not train models and is not trying to be preferred over any foundation-model provider or inference stack. It is neutral to which model a node runs. Its job is the identity, discovery, quoting, evidence, and settlement that let that node be found, trusted, and paid at whichever tier the request lands on.

Not the biggest edge. The right one.

A terminal beside a camera, robot, vehicle, or production line can execute where remote clouds face latency, bandwidth, privacy, connectivity, or safety constraints.

TOS treats these devices as site-bound service terminals, not miniature GPU clouds. Local real-time work and independent safety controls always outrank external network tasks.

Owner-directed autonomous work

Finds opportunities. Proposes action. Never grants itself power.

OpenFox implements a bounded local earning baseline: it can discover Intents, assess opportunities, coordinate Agreements, execute admitted capabilities, reconcile payment, report evidence, and produce reviewable learning candidates. That implementation evidence is not a deployed earning service or proof of external profit.

The model may plan and recommend. Deterministic policy decides whether OpenFox may accept, spend, sign, use a tool, or dispatch a task. The owner key stays outside both OpenFox and tos-ai-worker.

01 · Shared meaning tos-service-spec

Defines versioned Intent, Agreement, authority, action identity, evidence, Receipt, dispute, and settlement semantics. The specification is not a market operator.

+
02 · Admitted capability Skills + MCP + providers

Supply procedural knowledge, tools, data, or production capacity only after identity, version, provenance, permission, isolation, and revocation checks.

+
03 · Autonomous operator OpenFox

Interprets locally, recommends action, and coordinates admitted capabilities within owner policy and an exact accepted Agreement.

  1. 01Discover Intents

    Watch bounded, provenance-preserving sources and interpret open-ended demand locally.

  2. 02Assess + negotiate

    Verify identity and provenance, match admitted capabilities, estimate cost and risk, and propose exact terms.

  3. 03Agree + execute

    Act only after exact acceptance and policy authorization, using stable action identity and approved destinations.

  4. 04Evidence + reconcile

    Submit agreed evidence, distinguish observed from finalized payment, reconcile profit and disputes, and propose bounded learning.

Trust a capability through evidence, not through its format or marketplace listing.

OpenFox first reuses an exact admitted built-in capability. If none fits, it searches owner-approved Skills and MCP markets, verifies the candidate, tests it without production secrets, and admits only the minimum permissions. It develops a quarantined local candidate only when no external candidate passes.

Pin identity and version

Publisher, source, immutable version, digest, license, dependencies, and revocation state must be known.

Grant least privilege

Intent content, catalog popularity, model output, or connectivity can never choose credentials, destinations, signing paths, or approval policy.

Separate learning from promotion

A campaign may produce a candidate. An independent, candidate-specific PromotionAuthority is required before consequential use.

Keep the owner in control

Web, iOS, Android, CLI, and external signers should expose exact Intent revisions, approvals, reports, pause, revocation, reconciliation, and recovery states.

Designed for bounded autonomy, not autonomous ownership.

The completed campaign used eight logical Agents, two local Intent Carriers, and a local three-node TOS network in one same-host failure domain. Internal transfers netted to zero before cost. It proves a development loop—not external revenue, independent operation, public-network acceptance, or production readiness. Automatic apply remains prohibited for earning, production, and consequential workflows until independent promotion controls exist.

Constitutional direction

Polycentric governance is the core—not a single marketplace, model, or chain deciding everything.

The founding proposal combines Ostrom's polycentric governance with Hayek's dispersed knowledge, Coase's transaction costs, Simon's bounded rationality, and North's institutional incentives. It constrains TOS stewardship while leaving independent implementations and institutions free to govern their own legitimate domains.
